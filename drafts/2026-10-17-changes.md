## 2 · New section after 4: "Game statistics" (goes live only when Firebase Analytics is switched on in the game)

> **5. Game statistics**
> To see where the game is too hard or unclear, the Google Play version of the game sends anonymous game events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example, that a case was started or finished and with how many stars, which screen the game was on when it was closed, and which optional help or advertisement was used. These events contain no name, email, account or free text, and we have switched off the collection of your advertising identifier for them. Google may also record basic device and app information (such as phone model, Android version and app version) and an approximate region from the IP address. We see only totals. Google handles this data under its own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. In Europe and the UK this follows your consent choice in the game's settings.
