Privacy Policy

Port Sevrin: Nine Cases

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Port Sevrin: Nine Cases ("the game"), an Android game published by NOONE GAMES ("we", "us").

In short: we do not ask you to create an account, we do not ask for your name or email, and we do not run servers that store your information. Your progress is saved on your own phone. The only information leaving your device is collected by Google, to show advertisements and to process any purchase you choose to make, plus anything you choose to share yourself.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The game has no sign-in, no registration and no user accounts. We do not collect, receive or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress — completed cases, star ratings, coin balance and settings — in the app's own storage on your phone. This stays on your device.

Android may automatically back this file up to your Google account so your progress returns if you reinstall the game or set up a new phone. This is a standard Android feature called Auto Backup, controlled by you in your phone's settings, not by us. We cannot see or access those backups.

Uninstalling the game removes the save data from your device.

3. Advertising

The game shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To do this, Google may collect and process information including:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system version and app version
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • how you interact with advertisements

This is handled by Google under its own policies, not by us. We never receive your personal information from Google — only anonymous totals, such as how many advertisements were shown.

You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your phone's ads settings (on most phones Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).

4. In-app purchases

You can buy optional coin packs, or a one-time purchase that removes advertisements. Every payment is handled by Google Play, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never see your card details, bank details or billing address.

Google tells the game only that a purchase was completed, so it can add your coins or turn off ads. Google keeps a record of your purchases in your Google account, which you can see in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history. Refunds are requested through Google Play.

5. Advertising choices in Europe and the UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent screen before any personalised advertising, provided through Google's User Messaging Platform. You may accept or decline, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings screen. Declining does not restrict any part of the game.

6. Children

The game contains crime fiction and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The game's age rating is shown on its Google Play listing.

7. Sharing your warrant card

The game can make a picture of your warrant card (your rank and stars) for you to share. It is created on your phone and only leaves it if you tap Share and choose an app, such as WhatsApp or Instagram. That app then handles it under its own privacy policy. We never receive it.

8. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. We hold none to share.
